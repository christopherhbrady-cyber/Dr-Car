        Why Dr Car instead of a general chatbot?

        Dr Car follows a guided, safety-first workflow: symptom intake, danger screening, checks matched to skill level, repair-planning ranges, specialist lookup, and a printable mechanic report. It is a rule-based prototype, not a confirmed diagnosis.

        About Dr Car

        Dr Car is a class-project MVP designed to demonstrate a practical AI-enabled workflow for classic-car owners: structured intake → safety triage → iterative troubleshooting → DIY/professional decision → expertise matching → feedback.

        It is decision support, not a substitute for physical inspection by a qualified technician. Never rely on an AI-generated answer alone for a safety-critical repair.
